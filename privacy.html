Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 — what Hollow collects, why, and how to get your data removed.

This is a template policy for the Hollow bot and website. Have a lawyer review it before you treat it as final.

What we collect

Hollow only requests the Discord permissions it needs to function. Depending on the modules you enable, that includes:

  • Server data — server ID, channel IDs, role IDs, and enabled module settings.
  • User data — user IDs, roles, join dates, and XP/level progress.
  • Command data — which commands were run, by whom, and when, used for abuse prevention and analytics.
  • Moderation records — warnings, timeouts, kicks, and bans issued through Hollow.
  • Message content — only when a moderation feature explicitly requires it (for example sniping or auto-mod), and only long enough to perform that action.

What we never collect

  • Direct messages, unless a member voluntarily submits them for support.
  • Payment information — Hollow does not process payments on this website.
  • Passwords, emails, or government identifiers.

How we use it

  • To deliver command responses, moderation actions, and level progression.
  • To detect spam, raids, and abuse of the bot.
  • To maintain aggregate statistics such as server and command counts shown on this website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Ever.

Storage and retention

Operational data is stored on infrastructure provided by Discord, Vercel, and any database providers Hollow uses. Data is retained while your server uses the bot, and deleted within 30 days of Hollow being removed from your server or upon a valid deletion request.

Third parties

  • Discord — governed by the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and the Hollow API.
  • Integration providers (Spotify, YouTube, GitHub, and similar) — queried only when a member runs the relevant command.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Server owners can remove Hollow from their server to trigger automated deletion.

Contact

Open a ticket in the support server for any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data.
